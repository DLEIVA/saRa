## Review of remaining duplication

Mann-Whitney now loops over the selected outputs and uses shared dispatch plus one error-to-cell writer, replacing five repeated validation/population blocks. Numerical option accessors share parsing. Standalone/regression normality share calculation dispatch. Paired descriptives and independent effect indices share rank helpers. Analysis-specific plotting/rendering and table schemas remain separate; moving all of them into one generic helper would risk UI/lifecycle regressions without removing equivalent numerical duplication.
